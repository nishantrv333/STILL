Rules
  • Stay still and your flow builds. Flow is free speed.
  • Tap to jump the red spikes. Every tap breaks some of your flow.
  • A last-moment jump is a close call: it refunds most of the flow the tap cost.
  • Teal gates can't be jumped. Stay grounded with 35%+ flow and you pass straight through. Panic-tap into one and you stumble.
  • Miss an obstacle and you stumble: a brief slowdown and a lost life. You get 3 lives per race.
  • Reach 100% flow and you hit full flow — hold it as long as you can.
  • Win a race and the next one gets tougher: obstacles come more often and flow builds slower.
  • Lose all your lives, or lose the race, and your win streak resets to zero.
