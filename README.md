# STILL

A small racing game where staying calm makes you faster.

Most runner games reward you for tapping as much as possible. STILL does the opposite. Your speed comes from your flow, and flow only builds while you do nothing. Every tap costs you flow. The whole game is about learning when not to press the button.

You race against a rival called Echo. First one to the finish line wins.

## How to play

Open `inverse-race-v4.html` in any modern browser. That is it. No install, no server, no dependencies. It works on phones and desktops.

Tap the screen or press the space bar to jump.

## Rules

Stand still and your flow builds. Flow is free speed.

Red spikes must be jumped. Every jump breaks some of your flow. Jump at the last possible moment and you get most of that flow back.

Teal gates cannot be jumped. Stay on the ground with at least 35 percent flow and you pass straight through them. Panic and tap in front of one and you stumble.

Miss an obstacle and you stumble, lose a life and slow down for a moment. You get 3 lives per race.

Reach 100 percent flow and you hit full flow. Your aura turns gold. Try to hold it.

Win a race and the next one gets harder. Lose and your streak resets to zero. Win without losing a single life and the game calls it a perfect race.

## Difficulty

Easy has wide gaps and a slower rival. Medium is balanced. Hard is unforgiving and Echo is slightly faster than you, so you can only beat him by keeping your flow high.

## Settings

Dark and light themes, and a sound toggle. Your theme, sound choice and best streak are saved in your browser.
